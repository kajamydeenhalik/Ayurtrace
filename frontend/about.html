Our Mission

AyurTrace is a blockchain-powered traceability platform for herbal and Ayurvedic products. Our mission is transparency, sustainability, and trust from grower to consumer.

How It Works

Products are tracked through each stage — cultivation, processing, manufacturing — and finally verified by the consumer through QR codes.

Our Vision

To make herbal products fully transparent and trustworthy worldwide.
