About AyurTrace

AyurTrace is a blockchain-powered herbal traceability system that ensures authenticity, quality, and transparency of Ayurvedic herbs. Every step of the herb's journey is securely recorded—from geo-tagged cultivation and lab testing to processing and final product delivery.

End consumers can scan QR codes to verify provenance and ensure safe and ethical consumption.

Farmer / Grower

Responsibilities:

  • Register farm and define geo-fenced cultivation zones.
  • Upload soil test reports and select certified seeds.
  • Log cultivation and harvest activities directly on blockchain.

Blockchain Benefits: Transparency of farm location, soil health, and harvest history ensures sustainability and consumer trust.

Analyticore / Lab

Responsibilities:

  • Receive herb batches and perform quality testing.
  • Upload lab test results (moisture, pesticides, DNA tests) to blockchain.
  • Certify compliance with quality standards.

Blockchain Benefits: Immutable lab results ensure herbs are authentic and safe for all stakeholders.

Manufacturer

Responsibilities:

  • Process herbs into finished products (drying, grinding, packaging).
  • Generate QR codes linked to full supply-chain records.
  • Ensure quality compliance and sustainability via smart contracts.

Blockchain Benefits: Complete batch traceability, product authenticity verification for end consumers.
